## Research material

The tables in `data/` were compiled by AI research agents from public web sources in October 2026. They paraphrase the sources, quote at most short fragments, and link each row to its source. Source publishers keep their copyright. No images were downloaded. The compilation was not reviewed row by row; `docs/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` and `data/README.md` say what that means for how far to trust each table.

## Earlier study
